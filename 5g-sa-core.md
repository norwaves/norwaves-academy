# What is a 5G SA (Standalone) Core?

**Norwaves Academy | 5G Fundamentals | Concept 1**

[Back to Home](index)

---

## Overview

The 5G Standalone (SA) Core is the foundation of every private 5G 
network. Understanding the difference between Standalone and 
Non-Standalone 5G is essential for any organisation evaluating 
private network deployment.

---

## Two types of 5G

| Type | Full name | How it works |
|------|-----------|--------------|
| NSA | Non-Standalone | 5G radio built on top of existing 4G infrastructure. Faster than 4G but still dependent on it. |
| SA | Standalone | Pure 5G from radio to core. Fully independent. Enables the full capabilities of private 5G. |

Most public 5G networks today are NSA.
Every Norwaves private 5G deployment is built on SA.

---

## Why SA matters for your organisation

A Standalone 5G core unlocks capabilities that are simply not 
possible on NSA or Wi-Fi networks:

**Network slicing:** Divide one physical network into multiple 
virtual networks, each with guaranteed performance for a specific 
application or user group.

**Ultra-low latency:** Response times under 10 milliseconds, 
enabling real-time control of robotics, autonomous vehicles, and 
mission-critical systems.

**Full on-site control:** The entire network runs inside your 
facility. No dependency on a public operator. No data leaving 
your premises.

**Guaranteed quality of service:** Unlike Wi-Fi or public 5G, 
performance is guaranteed regardless of how many users or devices 
are connected.

---

## The practical difference

NSA is a 5G extension built on top of an existing 4G system.
SA is a completely independent 5G network you own and control.

For industrial applications -- robotics, surveillance, VR training, 
autonomous inspection -- only SA delivers the performance guarantees 
that operations depend on.

---

## Key terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| SA | Standalone -- pure 5G, no 4G dependency |
| NSA | Non-Standalone -- 5G radio on 4G core |
| 5G Core | The control centre of the network |
| gNB | The 5G radio unit connecting devices to the network |
| NPN | Non-Public Network -- a private 5G network built on SA core |
