## Overview

NPN stands for Non-Public Network. It is the official 3GPP 
standard term for what is commonly called a private 5G network. 
Every Norwaves deployment is an NPN -- a dedicated, secure, 
fully controlled 5G network built exclusively for one organisation.

---

## Public network vs NPN

| | Public 5G network | NPN (Private 5G) |
|-|-------------------|------------------|
| Who controls it | Telenor, Telia, Jio | Your organisation |
| Who shares it | Thousands of users | Only your people and devices |
| Where your data goes | Through operator infrastructure | Stays on your premises |
| Performance guarantee | Best effort | Guaranteed |
| Coverage | Wherever operator builds | Exactly where you need it |
| Dependency | On the operator | None |

---

## Two types of NPN

| Type | Full name | How it works |
|------|-----------|--------------|
| SNPN | Standalone Non-Public Network | Completely independent. Your own spectrum, your own 5G SA core, your own radio. No public operator involved. |
| PNI-NPN | Public Network Integrated NPN | Uses a public operator's infrastructure with a dedicated private slice for your organisation. |

Norwaves builds SNPNs -- fully standalone, fully owned by 
the customer. This is fundamentally different from purchasing 
a private slice from a public operator.
