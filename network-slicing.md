# What is Network Slicing?

Norwaves Academy | 5G Fundamentals | Concept 2

[Back to Home](index)

---

## Overview

Network slicing allows a single private 5G network to serve 
multiple applications simultaneously, each with its own 
guaranteed performance. It is one of the most powerful 
capabilities of 5G SA and a core reason why private 5G 
outperforms Wi-Fi for industrial environments.

---

## How it works

One physical network. Multiple virtual networks running on top 
of it. Each virtual network, or slice, is completely isolated 
from the others with its own dedicated resources and performance 
guarantees.

Think of it as a private motorway that your organisation owns. 
Different vehicles use it for different purposes, each with their 
own dedicated lane and guaranteed speed. A robot on one lane is 
never slowed down by a camera on another.

---

## The three slice types in 5G

| Type | Full name | Best for |
|------|-----------|----------|
| URLLC | Ultra Reliable Low Latency Communication | Robotics, VR, emergency services, defence |
| eMBB | Enhanced Mobile Broadband | Video surveillance, streaming, AR content |
| mMTC | Massive Machine Type Communication | IoT sensors, smart campus, asset tracking |

---

## A practical example

On a university campus private 5G network, three slices run 
simultaneously on the same physical infrastructure:

| Slice | Application | Guarantee |
|-------|-------------|-----------|
| URLLC | Robot dog and VR glasses | Latency under 10ms |
| eMBB | AI video surveillance cameras | High bandwidth for continuous video |
| mMTC | Student devices and IoT sensors | Wide coverage for many devices |

The robotics lab never slows down because students are online.
The surveillance cameras never drop frames because the robots 
are moving. Each application gets exactly what it needs.

---

## Why network slicing matters for your organisation

**Cost efficiency:** One private 5G network replaces multiple 
separate networks for different applications.

**Performance guarantee:** Each application gets dedicated 
resources, not shared bandwidth.

**Flexibility:** Slices can be created, modified, or removed 
as operational needs change.

**Security:** Each slice is completely isolated.
